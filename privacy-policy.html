Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 15, 2026

At Homesite Mortgage, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data in connection with our mortgage services.

We may collect personal information including, but not limited to:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
  • Financial information (e.g., income, credit history, loan details) provided during mortgage inquiries or applications.
  • Website usage data (e.g., IP address, browser type) via cookies and tracking technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide mortgage services, including pre-approvals and loan processing.
  • Communicate with you about your inquiries or services.
  • Improve our website and customer experience.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

We do not sell your personal data. It may be shared with trusted third parties (e.g., loan processors) only with your consent or as required by law.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze site performance, and serve personalized content. You can manage cookie preferences in your browser settings.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Homesite Mortgage uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to operate our website, understand how visitors use it, and (with your consent) deliver relevant advertising on third-party platforms.

We use the following categories of cookies:

  • Strictly Necessary: Required for the site to function. Cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics: Help us understand how visitors interact with the site.

You can manage your preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in our footer. We honor your choices on every page.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, such as encryption and secure servers, to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no online transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate data.

  • Opt out of marketing communications.

To exercise these rights, contact us at tcloans1@gmail.com.

We may disclose your information to:

  • Service providers assisting with mortgage processing (e.g., credit bureaus).
  • Legal authorities if required by law or to protect our rights.

We also use the following third-party service providers, which process limited information on our behalf to operate our website, communicate with you, and manage inquiries:

These providers are authorized to use your information only as needed to perform services for us. We do not sell your personal information.
