# READ THIS BEFORE EDITING. A crawler obeys exactly ONE group: the most specific
# one whose token matches it (RFC 9309 s2.2.1). It does NOT fall through to
# `User-agent: *` afterwards. Giving a crawler its own group therefore EXEMPTS it
# from every rule in the `*` group. That is precisely how nineteen near-duplicate
# city pages stayed open to every AI crawler for weeks while appearing blocked.
#
# So: any rule that must apply to a named crawler has to be repeated inside that
# crawler's own group. Multiple User-agent lines may share one group, which is
# why the tokens are stacked below instead of repeated nineteen times each.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
