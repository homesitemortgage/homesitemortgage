Common Questions

Questions Florida Buyers Ask Us

Do I need to visit an office to get a mortgage with Homesite Mortgage?

No — Homesite Mortgage is a fully online brokerage. You can complete everything from prequalification to closing by phone, email, and secure online tools, without an office visit. A real person handles your loan personally the whole way.

What areas of Florida does Homesite Mortgage serve?

We're based in Melbourne and serve all of Brevard County and the Space Coast — including Palm Bay, Viera, Titusville, Rockledge, Cocoa, Merritt Island, and Satellite Beach — as well as borrowers across the state of Florida.

Will starting my prequalification affect my credit?

No. Starting your prequalification with us does not require a hard credit pull, and there's no obligation to move forward.

What types of home loans do you offer?

Purchase, refinance, FHA, VA, conventional, DSCR/investment, and non-QM (self-employed) loans. If you're not sure which fits, we'll review your situation and tell you what you qualify for.

How do I get started?

Start your free prequalification online in about 5 minutes, or call us at 321-751-4403. A real person reviews every submission and follows up personally.

How do I know if I qualify?

The best way to find out is to start a free prequalification — it takes about 5 minutes and doesn't require a hard credit pull. We review your situation and tell you what you may qualify for, with no obligation either way.

What does it cost to work with a broker?

There's no cost or obligation to get prequalified and explore your options. As a broker, we shop your loan across multiple lenders to find a fit for your situation, and we'll walk you through any costs clearly and upfront before you commit to anything.
