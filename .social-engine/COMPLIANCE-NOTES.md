# Social post compliance — Homesite Mortgage

Every Facebook post that promotes Homesite is a mortgage **advertisement** under Florida law and federal rules. Follow ALL of these — a violation must never be posted:

- **No "triggering terms" (numbers).** No specific interest rate, APR, monthly payment, loan term, down-payment amount or percentage, or phrases like "X% down / no money down / zero down / $0 down." Under Reg Z (12 CFR 1026.24) any such term forces a full disclosure block — so keep numbers out of social entirely and send people to a prequal instead.
- **No promises of a result.** No "as low as," "lowest rates," "guaranteed approval," "guaranteed rates," or implied savings.
- **No invented testimonials.** Only quote the real Google reviews provided, using the client's exact words and first name only.
- **No specific fees, costs, or savings claims.**
- **Always honest and non-misleading.** Equal Housing Opportunity. NMLS #353790.
- **People-first, never hard-sell.** We build trust; we invite, we don't pressure.
- **Soft CTA only:** start a free prequalification (no hard credit pull, no obligation) at homesitemortgage.online, or call 321-751-4403.

Every drafted post is archived as a dated file in `/social-drafts` to satisfy Florida's 2-year mortgage-ad recordkeeping rule. **Posting is manual, after human approval — nothing auto-posts.**
