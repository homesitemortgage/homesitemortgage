Accessibility Statement

Effective Date: June 21, 2026

Homesite Mortgage is committed to making our website, homesitemortgage.online, accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We believe every visitor — regardless of ability — deserves an equal opportunity to learn about home financing and reach our team.

Our Standard

We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, the internationally recognized standard referenced by the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA). These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, hearing, cognitive, and motor impairments.

What We Have Done

Accessibility was built into this website from the start. Measures in place include:

  • Screen-reader support — meaningful page structure, descriptive headings, descriptive text alternatives ("alt text") on images, and labels on form fields so assistive technology can read the page aloud.
  • Keyboard navigation — every interactive element can be reached and used with a keyboard alone, with a "Skip to main content" link and visible focus outlines for people who do not use a mouse.
  • Readable color contrast — text and background colors meet or exceed the WCAG AA contrast ratio so they remain legible for people with low vision or color blindness.
  • Reduced motion — animations are minimized for visitors whose devices request reduced motion, which helps people with vestibular (balance) disorders.
  • Resizable text and responsive layout — the site reflows cleanly when text is enlarged or viewed on a phone, tablet, or desktop.
  • Plain, clear language — we aim to explain mortgage topics simply and avoid unnecessary jargon.

Compatibility

This website is designed to work with recent versions of major browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge) and with common assistive technologies, including screen readers and browser zoom/magnification.

Ongoing Effort

We treat accessibility as an ongoing commitment, not a one-time project. We review the site as we add content and work to fix any barriers we discover.
