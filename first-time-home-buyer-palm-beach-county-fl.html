Buying Your First Home in Palm Beach County

You Don't Have to Figure This Out Alone

Palm Beach County stretches from the Atlantic coastline inland through diverse communities — the urban energy of West Palm Beach, the established neighborhoods of Boca Raton, the family-friendly feel of Boynton Beach, and the equestrian community of Wellington. For first-time buyers, having this range of communities and price points to consider is both exciting and a little overwhelming.

The hardest part of buying a first home usually isn't the paperwork — it's not knowing what happens next. How do you figure out your real budget? Which loan fits your situation? What does a prequalification actually mean to a seller in a competitive market? This guide answers those questions in order, the same way we'd walk through them with you on a call.

Wherever you're looking in Palm Beach County, the path to your first closing follows the same general sequence — and understanding it up front takes a lot of the stress out of the journey.

The First-Time Buyer Process

Five Steps From "Maybe" to Move-In

  • 1. Get prequalified. A free prequal gives you a realistic budget before you start shopping, so you're looking at homes you can actually finance — not falling in love with one you can't. It also signals to sellers in a competitive Palm Beach County market that you're a serious buyer.
  • 2. Understand your loan options. First-time buyers in Florida often consider FHA, VA, or conventional loans — and may also qualify for down payment assistance. We'll walk through the trade-offs and help you understand which paths may fit your situation.
  • 3. Find a home and make an offer. With a prequal in hand, your offer carries more weight. A local real estate agent and a Florida-licensed lender working together makes this step smoother and faster.
  • 4. Underwriting and the appraisal. Your file is reviewed and the home is appraised. We manage the documentation and keep you updated throughout — no surprises at the finish line.
  • 5. Close and get your keys. You review and sign your final documents, and the home is yours. Our team processes and closes your loan in-house.

Timelines vary by situation and market conditions, but knowing the sequence helps you plan with confidence from the very first step.

Loan Programs First-Time Florida Buyers Often Use

Common Paths to a First Home

FHA Loans

Insured by the Federal Housing Administration, FHA loans offer flexible credit guidelines and are designed for buyers who don't have a large amount saved. They're one of the most common starting points for first-time buyers across Florida. Learn more →

VA Loans

For eligible veterans, active service members, and certain surviving spouses, VA loans offer exclusive benefits and favorable financing terms. Eligibility depends on your service history and VA guidelines. Learn more →

Conventional Loans

Not government-insured, conventional loans come with their own low-down-payment options for qualified buyers and can offer flexibility in terms of property types and loan structures. Often a strong fit when credit and savings are in a solid place. Learn more →

Down Payment Assistance

Florida offers assistance programs that may help eligible first-time buyers with down payment or closing costs. Availability and eligibility rules change frequently — we'll check what's current for your situation and area.

Down Payment Help in Florida

Assistance Programs Worth Asking About

One of the most common reasons people delay buying a home is the belief that they need a large down payment saved before they can even start. For many first-time buyers in Palm Beach County, that's not the full picture. Beyond the low-down-payment loan programs described above, the Florida Housing Finance Corporation and various local and county-level programs periodically offer down payment and closing-cost assistance to eligible buyers.

These programs carry their own income limits, eligibility criteria, and funding cycles — and they change over time. We don't want to quote specifics that might be out of date by the time you read this. What we can do is review your situation and check which programs are currently available and which you may qualify for. If there's assistance out there for a buyer in your position, we'll find it.

First-Time Buyer Questions

Answers to What People Ask Us Most

Do I have to be a true first-time buyer to qualify for assistance in Palm Beach County?

Not necessarily. Many programs define a first-time buyer as someone who hasn't owned a primary residence in the past three years, so even if you've owned a home before, you may still qualify. Some assistance programs also have provisions for buyers in certain areas regardless of prior ownership. Rules change, so we check the current requirements for your specific situation.

Which loan type is best for a first-time buyer in Palm Beach County?

It depends on your financial picture and eligibility. FHA loans are designed for buyers who want flexible credit guidelines and a lower barrier to entry. VA loans offer exclusive benefits for eligible veterans and service members. Conventional loans can be a strong fit when credit and savings are in a solid place. We review your situation and explain which options may work best for you — there's no single right answer.

Is down payment assistance available to Palm Beach County buyers?

Programs may be available through the Florida Housing Finance Corporation and local sources. Availability, eligibility rules, and funding levels change over time, so we don't quote specifics that might be outdated. What we can do is review your situation and check which current programs you may qualify for — if there's help available, we'll find it.
